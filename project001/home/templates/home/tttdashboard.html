Tic Tac Toe: Think Fast, Play Smart!

    
Tic Tac Toe (also known as Noughts and Crosses) is a classic two-player strategy game played on a 3×3 grid. Players take turns marking a space in the grid with their symbol X or O. The goal is to be the first to get three of your symbols in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally!
